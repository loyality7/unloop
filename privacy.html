Unloop privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-15.

Short version

Unloop works entirely on your phone. It has no internet permission, no account, no ads and no analytics. Nothing about you or your screen is sent anywhere.

What Unloop looks at

With your permission, Android's Accessibility service shows Unloop the structure of screens in the apps it supports and in apps you set limits on: names and positions of buttons and views, and which tab is open. It uses this only to recognise Reels, Shorts and similar screens so it can go back, tap a tab or cover a button. It does not read what you type, passwords or messages, and it never reads payment or banking apps. Screen content is not stored or sent.

What stays on your phone

Your settings, limits, schedules, groups, daily usage totals per app (from Android usage access), and counters such as how many times Unloop stepped back. They are kept in the app's private storage, are excluded from Android cloud backup, and are deleted by Settings > Backup > Delete all local data or by uninstalling.

Permissions and why

  • Accessibility service: to recognise the screens above.
  • Usage access: to measure time per app for limits and insights.
  • Notification access (optional): to snooze noisy notifications from apps you choose. Message-style notifications are left alone and notification text is not stored.
  • Notifications: to show a calm limit reminder.
  • Run at startup: to restore schedules after a restart.

Optional: Learn mode

If you turn on Learn mode, Unloop saves the layout of screens you choose to capture (view names, sizes and positions) on your phone. Text is left out unless you separately switch on "Include on-screen text". Nothing leaves your phone unless you copy it out yourself.

Backup

Backup copies your rules to the clipboard as plain text. Nothing is uploaded.

Children

Unloop is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version ships with the app. Questions: write to sarath@gainandshine.com, or open an issue in the project repository.
